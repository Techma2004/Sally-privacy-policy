Privacy Policy

Privacy policy for Sally Bot

This Privacy Policy explains how Sally Bot collects, uses, stores, protects, and shares information when you interact with the bot, including Telegram conversations, memory features, and AI-powered responses.

Last updated: September 22, 2026 Applies to Telegram users User-controlled data settings

1. Overview

Sally Bot is designed to provide helpful, personalized, and context-aware AI responses. To do that, we may process information you provide when using the bot, including messages, usernames, and optional memory data saved by you.

We are committed to using this information responsibly and only for the purposes described in this policy. We do not sell personal data. We also do not collect sensitive personal information unless you voluntarily provide it in a message or memory entry.

We collect only the information necessary to operate the bot, improve the user experience, and maintain service reliability.

2. Information We Collect

  • User messages sent to Sally Bot for processing and response generation.
  • User ID, username, and account details provided by Telegram or the platform hosting the bot.
  • Conversation content needed to maintain context and provide relevant responses.
  • Optional memory or saved preferences that you choose to store.
  • Technical and operational data such as usage patterns, logs, and error reports used to improve performance.

We do not intentionally collect highly sensitive personal information such as government-issued IDs, payment details, or private documents unless a user explicitly and knowingly provides such information during a conversation.

3. How We Use Information

  • To generate responses, provide conversation continuity, and support personalized interaction.
  • To maintain memory features and save user-defined data when enabled.
  • To improve bot quality, reliability, and performance over time.
  • To diagnose bugs, fix issues, and maintain system security.
  • To comply with legal obligations or respond to valid user requests.

4. Data Storage and Retention

User data may be stored locally, in a secure database, or on a server environment controlled by the bot operator, depending on the deployment setup. Memory data is kept only when you explicitly save it or enable such functionality.

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide the service, meet legal requirements, or support legitimate operational needs. If you request deletion, we will process that request in a reasonable and timely manner, subject to any legal or technical limitations.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade user data with third parties for marketing or unrelated purposes. However, data may be processed by the following categories of service providers when required to operate the bot:

  • AI model providers responsible for generating responses.
  • Hosting, cloud, or infrastructure providers supporting the bot.
  • Security, debugging, and technical support services.

These parties are expected to handle data in a secure and responsible manner and only as required for service operation.

6. Your Rights and Control Options

You have control over how your data is used. Depending on the bot’s configuration, you may be able to:

  • Request deletion of stored data.
  • Clear conversation history.
  • Disable or limit memory features.
  • Stop using the bot at any time.
  • Ask questions about how your information is handled.

If you want to exercise these rights, contact the bot developer or use the controls provided within the bot where available.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. These may include access controls, secure hosting, encrypted communication, and regular monitoring.

No digital system is completely risk-free. While we take precautions, users should avoid sending highly sensitive or private information that they would not want a third party to access.

8. Children’s Privacy

Sally Bot is intended for general use and may be used by individuals of varying ages. If you are under the age threshold required by applicable law, use the bot only with parental consent or guidance where required. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children for marketing or unrelated purposes.

9. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the bot, legal requirements, or user expectations. When changes are made, the “Last updated” date at the top of this policy will be revised. Continued use of Sally Bot after the update indicates acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Important Notice

AI-generated responses are produced based on the information you provide and the systems used to process it. While we aim to provide useful and safe outputs, automated responses may occasionally be incomplete, inaccurate, or contextually limited.

Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the Sally Bot developer through the official Telegram support channel or the project’s support contact associated with the bot.

You may also request information about your stored data or ask for deletion through the bot’s available controls or support options.
